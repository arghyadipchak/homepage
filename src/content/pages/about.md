I am a Master of Science by Research student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay), working in the [SynerG Lab](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/synerg/) advised by [Prof. Mythili Vutukuru](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~mythili/).

My research broadly focuses on Networked Systems, with a particular interest in 5G/6G Cellular Networks, Congestion Control, and Internet Measurement. I am currently investigating in-RAN control mechanisms for 5G that reduce queue buildup and RTT inflation under standard end-host congestion control algorithms.

Prior to IIT Bombay, I completed my Master of Science in Computer Science at Chennai Mathematical Institute (CMI), and earned my Bachelor of Mathematics (Honours) from the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) Bangalore.
